overview: This platform is designed for online exam taking, primarily aimed at independent educators looking to assess their students. Its main goal is to make the most of the time students spend at home. We have focused on creating a clean user interface and an accessible experience for teachers.
problem: A significant issue with similar applications has been their accessibility and modern user interface, particularly regarding anti-cheating measures. Other platforms often had a complicated test creation process, which posed a challenge, and they also lacked the ability to view test history.
whatIDid: I have introduced a modern user interface that enhances accessibility and user experience. Teachers can now easily access all tests, including history and ongoing assessments, and track individual student progress. The anti-cheating flagging system also promotes transparency in the testing process.
