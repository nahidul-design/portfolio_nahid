overview: This platform connects three key players — the sender who wants to ship a parcel, the carrier responsible for delivery, and the receiver eagerly awaiting the package. We've created a space that links everyone together, allowing users to track their parcel's journey.
problem: The challenge was balancing cost and urgency. Traditional overseas delivery services often have longer delivery times and higher costs. Our solution enables frequent travelers to utilize their extra luggage space, earning some extra cash while promoting efficient and eco-friendly use of space on flights.
whatIDid: I've designed a modern user interface focused on usability for this multi-role platform. The process is straightforward, allowing users to easily find a suitable traveler, track their courier, and connect with the receiver seamlessly.
